# Playlist Curation Guides

These guides define preferences for this project's small song catalog.
They reflect design choices and may not suit every listener.

## Workout

- Target energy is 0.90 on the catalog's 0–1 scale.
- Minimum energy is 0.75.
- Suitable mood tags are happy, energizing, intense, aggressive,
  playful, and uplifting.
- Prefer energizing tracks when other preferences are equal.
- Use catalog metadata to explain each recommendation.
- Ask for approval before relaxing an explicit listener constraint.

## Studying

- Target energy is 0.35 on the catalog's 0–1 scale.
- Maximum energy is 0.50.
- Suitable mood tags are chill, focused, relaxed, and peaceful.
- Prefer instrumental tracks when other preferences are equal.
- Preserve any genre explicitly requested by the listener.
- Ask for approval before broadening the search.

## Recommendation Evidence

- Recommend only tracks present in the retrieved catalog records.
- Support explanations with the retrieved attributes and scoring results.
- Identify the guide section used for the request.
- Tell the listener when fewer tracks match than requested.
